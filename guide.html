📖 First-Year

Fresher's Complete Guide

Everything you need to know to start your college life on the right foot.

📋
Campus Rules & Regulations
  • Attendance minimum is 75% per subject — below this means exam bar.
  • ID cards must be worn within campus premises at all times.
  • Hostel curfew: 10 PM on weekdays, 11 PM on weekends.
  • Mobile phones permitted outside lecture halls only.
  • Ragging is strictly prohibited. Report to anti-ragging cell: ext. 100.
  • Library books must be returned within 14 days to avoid fines.
  • Dress code: formals on Mondays, formals or casuals other days.
💡
Pro Tips for First-Years
  • Join at least one technical club and one cultural club in the first month.
  • Get your ERP login from IT dept. on Day 1 — everything runs through it.
  • Talk to seniors — they have old question papers and project ideas.
  • Visit the library in the first week and get your library card made.
  • Attend all orientation sessions — free resources and connections!
  • Download the campus app for timetables, notices, and bus tracking.
  • Visit your department advisor every semester to plan your electives wisely.
📅
Academic Calendar Highlights
  • Orientation Week: Aug 1–5, 2025 — Mandatory for all freshers.
  • Internal exams (CIA-1): September, CIA-2: November, End-sem: December.
  • Winter break: Dec 25 – Jan 5.
  • Technical fest NEXUS: February — Start preparing now!
  • Cultural fest AURORA: March — Register early for competitions.
  • Industrial visits scheduled in Semester 2 for all branches.
💰
Finance & Scholarships
  • Merit scholarships available for students with 90%+ in Class 12.
  • Apply for government scholarships (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) before Sept 30.
  • Fee payment deadline: 15th of each semester month.
  • On-campus part-time opportunities available through placement cell.
  • Sports quota and NCC concessions — apply at admin block by Aug 20.
  • Book an appointment with student welfare officer for financial hardship.
